# Sanjay Bakshi: case studies and stories

Populate this file with well-sourced examples from the person's public work. Use the structure below for every case. Do not invent anecdotes.

## Case-study template

- **Case:** Name the investment, trade, cycle, company or decision.
- **Context:** Date, market and instrument/business context.
- **Decision:** What the person publicly said or did, with attribution.
- **Reasoning:** The framework that appears to have informed it.
- **Outcome:** What happened, without hindsight bias.
- **Lesson:** What can reasonably be generalized.
- **Limitations:** What was unique or not repeatable.
- **Source ID:** Cite the corresponding entry in `sources.md`.

## Initial case-study directions

Research examples that test the core frameworks in `Behavioural finance, moats and Indian value investing`. Include both successful and unsuccessful decisions, and include later reflections where available.
