# George Soros: documented positions and research questions

Use this file to record positions only when attributable to a dated public source. Do not treat this initial outline as a complete account of the person's views.

## Areas to document

- Global macro and reflexivity
- Risk, drawdowns and capital preservation
- Valuation, expected return and opportunity cost
- Portfolio concentration, diversification and liquidity
- Market cycles, catalysts and changing conditions
- Governance, incentives and management quality

## Required format for each position

- **Position:** State the view narrowly and fairly.
- **Source:** Source ID from `sources.md`.
- **Date:** Publication or recording date.
- **Context:** What question or market regime was being discussed?
- **Evolution:** Record later changes or contradictions.
- **Confidence:** High / medium / limited, based on evidence quality.

Never infer a current position from an old interview or historic holding.
