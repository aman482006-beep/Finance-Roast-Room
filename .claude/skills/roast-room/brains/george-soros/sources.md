## Evidence standard

- Prefer shareholder letters, official annual reports, original books, lectures and full interviews.
- Use reputable financial journalism as secondary corroboration.
- Record publication/recording date and the exact claim supported.
- Avoid quote aggregators, anonymous social posts, scraped portfolios and unverified video snippets as sole evidence.
- For current investment activity, use current regulatory filings and issuer disclosures, not this historical source list.
